================================================================================
RECORDING PROCEDURE
================================================================================
1. Source workspace:
   source install/setup.bash

2. Launch simulation (example baseline):
   ros2 launch hunav_gazebo_wrapper central_tunnel_no_robot.launch.py

3. Launch with specific config:
   ros2 launch hunav_gazebo_wrapper central_tunnel_no_robot.launch.py \
     configuration_file:=domenic/central_tunnel/ct_phase1_social_low.yaml

4. Record bag (in separate terminal):
   ros2 bag record -a -o results/central_tunnel/ct_baseline_run1

5. Wait for simulation to complete (agents traverse tunnel ~30m)

6. Run evaluation:
   ros2 launch hunav_evaluator hunav_evaluator_launch.py

7. Compare with ground truth:
   python3 src/DATASETS/scripts/ground_truth_analysis.py \
     --bag results/central_tunnel/ct_baseline_run1 \
     --ground-truth src/DATASETS/eth/univ/
